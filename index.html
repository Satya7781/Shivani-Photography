Behind the photographs.

I'm Shivani, a wedding photographer based in Bhopal. I love the quiet moments as much as the celebrations: a nervous smile before the ceremony, a parent's hand resting on a shoulder, and the way two people look at each other when nobody is watching.

I photograph weddings with patience and curiosity, leaving room for the day to unfold naturally. My aim is to give you photographs that feel like the day itself, even years after it has passed.

Kind words, kept close.

A few things worth knowing.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular wedding dates can fill several months ahead, so reaching out 6–12 months before your wedding is a good place to start. If your date is closer, please still ask.

Do you travel outside Bhopal?

Yes. I photograph weddings across India and am happy to discuss destination celebrations. Travel and accommodation arrangements are included in your final customized quotation.

When will we receive our photographs?

Your final edited gallery is typically delivered within 6–8 weeks after the wedding. Larger multi-day celebrations may require additional time, which we will discuss in advance.
